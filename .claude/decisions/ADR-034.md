# ADR-034 — The signature look is held quiet: no metal shine, no specular spot, a low glow

- **Status:** Superseded by [ADR-035](./ADR-035.md); earlier Proposed — the owner asked for it in session, 2026-09-24 ("The ai interface is too glowy and
  metalic, reduce these effects so we have more consistent look on our app"); this text awaits acceptance
- **Date:** 2026-09-24
- **Deciders:** Andre, Architect
- **Relates to:** [ADR-026](./ADR-026.md) — "a lighter, more translucent background"; the theme contract in
  `packages/embed/src/theme.ts` (every value derived from the adopter's page).

## Context

The widget's look was "glass, metal and light": translucent surfaces with a bright top-left edge, primary
buttons as polished metal (a lit gradient, a white conic rim, an inset highlight, a sweep of light on
hover), a saturated blur behind glass, an aura and a white specular spot on the orb, and a composer that
glowed as it breathed. On Pilot-A — flat surfaces, soft shadows, a restrained accent — it read as an object
laid over the app rather than part of it.

## Decision

Every effect stays derived from the page's scheme and accent; only its strength changes.

- **Surfaces:** glass 0.74 / 0.78 opaque (dark / light), strong glass 0.92 / 0.94; the lit edge 0.08 on
  a dark page (was 0.26); no `saturate()` in any backdrop filter.
- **Primary buttons:** the accent, filled, shaded ±8% (was +38% / −32%); no conic rim, no inset
  highlight, no sweep of light; a 1–2px shadow.
- **Glow and aurora:** the accent at 0.16 / 0.12 (was 0.5 / 0.38), aurora at 0.08 and 0.04.
- **Orb:** aura 0.04 + 0.08 × level (was 0.12 + 0.2 × level); the hot centre of each mass at 0.16
  (was 0.42); no specular highlight; a dark page's masses at 0.86 density.
- **Composer beam:** the resting breath is a 1px inset line at 0.06–0.22 with no outer glow; the thinking
  bloom at 0.3; the voice glow at 0.25 + 0.35 × level.

## Consequences

- States still read — the beam still travels while thinking and rises with the voice — just lower.
- The theme tests' relationships (edge lit above dim, metal lighter above darker below, glass translucent)
  still hold; only magnitudes moved.
