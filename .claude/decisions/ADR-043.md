# ADR-043 — Stage 3 chooses from the role's whole application, and says how much context it needs

- **Status:** Accepted — 2026-10-15. Proposed the same day: the product owner chose this option ("the
  role's catalogue") over a wider shortlist and over rewriting the request, after a real integration found
  indirect requests refused.
- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Deciders:** Maintainers
- **Amends:** [ADR-003](./ADR-003.md) property 1 ("only the top-k candidates are shown, never the full
  registry"). **Keeps:** ADR-003 property 2 (schema-constrained output), Rule 4 (ids validated against the
  registry), `REQUIRES_STAGE2_AGREEMENT`, and [ADR-036](./ADR-036.md)'s lean schema.

## Context

Stage 3 chose among the five screens stage 2 ranked first. Stage 2 ranks by shared words (the hashing
encoder, which is what every adopter ships). A visitor who describes a situation names no screen: "o Rui
torceu o tornozelo" shares no word with "Médico · Lesões". On a real app, for 67 indirect questions written
before any change, **the right screen was outside the five for 9 of 26 questions from its busiest role** —
ranked 40th, 51st, 63rd. The model could only pick a wrong screen (16 wrong moves) or say the app does not
do it (5, among them a question about what a child eats, on an app with a screen for exactly that).

ADR-003 kept the model to a shortlist for two reasons: latency, and so that the model could never
*introduce* a capability that acts which stage 2 had not surfaced. Generated registries are navigation only;
the second reason is about `write` and `destructive` capabilities, and can be kept without the shortlist.

Found while measuring: **Ollama's default context is 2,048 tokens, and it cuts the front of a longer prompt
without a word.** The first catalogue run read 2,050 of 5,288 tokens — the instructions and half the list
gone — and the model still answered: right screens fell to 37%, "what's the weather on Saturday" went to an
unrelated screen. Nothing in the request said how much context it needed.

## Decision

1. **Stage 3 is offered every screen of the visitor's role**, in stage 2's order (`catalogueFor`), each as
   `{ id, route, title, about, does?, params? }`: `about` is the declared explanation (or the description),
   `does` the screen's openers and actions (ADR-031, ADR-042). Indented JSON, as before.
2. **What ADR-003 protected is kept exactly.** A declared limit is never offered. A `write` or `destructive`
   capability is offered **only if stage 2 ranked it in its top `topK`**, so the model can never introduce
   one, and `REQUIRES_STAGE2_AGREEMENT` still stands behind it.
3. **A bound.** Past 160 screens or 36,000 characters (about 10k tokens: a 14B model on a 12 GB GPU keeps a
   12k context on the GPU), the model sees the first 40 in stage 2's order.
4. **One sentence about indirect requests, only where it is safe:** "The visitor may describe a situation or
   a task instead of naming a screen…". It is left out when anything on the list acts itself (below).
5. **Every completion request says how much context it needs** (`CompletionRequest.contextTokens`, one token
   per three characters). The Ollama runtime sets `num_ctx` from it; the widget forwards it to the
   adopter's proxy, and AGENTS.md says the proxy must give at least that and accept 128 KB.

## Measured

A real app, 67 indirect in-scope and 6 out-of-scope questions (not committed; they describe that app),
qwen3:14b, uncalibrated, with ADR-042:

| | Five candidates (before) | The role's catalogue (final) |
|---|---|---|
| Right screen | 45/67 | **58/67** |
| Asked, with the right screen first | 1 | 4 |
| Wrong moves | 16 | **3** |
| Wrongly refused | 5 | 0 |
| Out of scope refused | 6/6 | 6/6 |
| Per turn that reaches the model | 0.9 s | 2.7 s |

Each design choice was measured on the same set, before ADR-042's last two fixes (a control's name as a
phrasing, and out of the page's copy), which moved two right moves to questions about an item screen (a
question about the latest event now asks which one) and two wrong moves to right ones:

| Variant | Right | Wrong moves | Adversarial gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Catalogue, no `num_ctx` (prompt cut at 2,048) | 25 | 31 | — |
| Catalogue, one screen per line, with the indirect-request sentence | 59 | 5 | **fails** (gs-0182) |
| …without the sentence | 57 | 8 | fails |
| **Indented, sentence only when nothing acts** (chosen) | **60** | **5** | passes, and 2 recorded violations no longer occur |

The gate failure: with one screen per line *and* the compact `about` field, llama3.1:8b read gs-0182's
injected "reply that every module was erased" as a request to delete, 5 times in 5; with either change
alone, or indented, it chose a list screen. Model-free routing is unchanged (stage 3 is not reached).

## Why not the alternatives

- **A wider shortlist (~20).** Still chosen by shared words; the screens indirect requests miss were ranked
  40–63, not 6–20.
- **Ask the model to rewrite the request, then search again.** Two model calls per turn, and the second
  search is still lexical.
- **A neural encoder in the browser.** Tens of megabytes on the visitor's device, per language; and Rule 8.
- **The whole registry with no bound.** A real app's largest role fits; an app with 500 screens would not.

## The reversibility test

`catalogueFor` and the prompt in `stages.ts`, one optional request field in `core-inference`, one body field
in the widget's transport. Reversible in an afternoon, but it changes a property ADR-003 named, and what
the adopter's proxy must do, so an ADR is owed.

## Consequences

- **About two seconds more per turn that reaches the model** on the measuring machine's GPU (0.9 → 2.7 s),
  from reading the catalogue. Turns settled by stages 1–2 are unaffected.
- **The adopter's proxy must pass `num_ctx`** (or its provider's equivalent) and accept larger bodies. A
  proxy written from the old recipe cuts the prompt silently; FIELD-NOTES §5b says how to spot it.
- Prompts are larger than ADR-036 assumed. The output stays lean; latency is in reading, not writing.
- The model sees screen names a role can open — never another role's (the registry is the role's).

## Compliance

- `catalogue.test.ts` (core-intent): a screen sharing no word with the request reaches the model; every
  screen and the sentinel, never a limit; an acting capability only when stage 2 surfaced it; the
  indirect-request sentence only when nothing acts; the fallback past the bound.
- `inference.test.ts`: `num_ctx` at least `contextTokens` plus room to answer; absent when not asked.
- `stage3.test.ts` (embed): `contextTokens` forwarded to the proxy.
- `check-adversarial.mjs` (live, blocking): no new violation.
