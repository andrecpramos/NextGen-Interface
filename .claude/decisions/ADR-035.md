# ADR-035 — The panel has a Chat view and a Tutorials view, the surface is flat, and the orb sits centred

- **Status:** Accepted — by the owner, in session, 2026-09-25. Proposed 2026-09-24: the owner asked for it ("Improve the tutorials ux inside the
  orb chat. If i change the screen while the chat is open the tutorials update accordingly. I want to be
  able to go back to chat mode at ease … Centre the orb in the mini chat … reduce drasticly the glows and
  the metalic feel")
- **Date:** 2026-09-24
- **Deciders:** Andre, Architect
- **Supersedes:** [ADR-034](./ADR-034.md) (the quiet look), which did not go far enough.
- **Amends:** [ADR-033](./ADR-033.md) — the options row and the tours shown as a message.

## Context

ADR-033 showed the tours as a message in the conversation. A message is a snapshot: change page and it
still lists the old page's tours, and getting back to the conversation meant scrolling past it. ADR-034
lowered the glow and the metal, and on the owner's screen it still read as glowing and metallic: the
aurora across the top of the panel, the bevelled edges, the composer's breathing outline, and an orb shaded
like a polished sphere with glittering dots. In the empty panel the orb sat low, dropped onto the greeting.

## Decision

**1. Two views, as tabs above the composer: Chat and Tutorials.** Tutorials shows the page's tours — its
name, what page they are for, one row per tour with where the visitor left it — or, when there are none,
says so and that another screen may have some. The conversation is kept while the tours show. Back to
it: the Chat tab, Escape, or typing in the composer. The arrow keys move between the tabs.

**2. The view follows the page.** It is read again when shown, after every move this assistant makes,
on the browser's own back and forward, and whenever the app calls the new handle method `refresh()` —
for a single-page app's own router, and when a tour's progress changes.

**3. "tutorial" opens the Tutorials view** when there is more than one tour, and still starts the only
one at once.

**4. The companion's `open()` is a header icon again**, as ADR-026 had it. An app that has no separate
assistant to open passes no `open`, and there is none.

**5. The surface is flat.** Every surface is the page's raised colour, all but opaque, edged with the
page's border colour — no lit edge, no aurora, no glow, no metal (`--ng-edge-*`, `--ng-glow`,
`--ng-aurora-*` are `transparent`; `--ng-metal-*` are the accent). Corner controls are plain icon
buttons. The composer at rest is a field like the app's; only thinking and voice light it, low. The orb
has no aura, no off-centre lighting and no `screen` blending; its dots are at 55% strength.

**6. In the empty panel, the orb and the greeting are centred** in the space above the composer.

## Consequences

- Script-tag adopters cannot call `refresh()`; their page changes are full loads, which read the page
  anyway, and history moves are followed.
- The theme variables keep their names so an adopter's override still resolves; their values are plain.

## Addendum — 2026-09-24: the chat box is round

The owner asked for a round border on the chat ("apply round border to the chat"). The composer took the
page's own field radius and its send button the page's button radius, so on an app with square fields
(Pilot-A: a few pixels) the box was a rectangle with a square button. The composer is now a pill
(`border-radius: 999px`) and the buttons inside it are round, whatever the page's radii. This is the one
place the widget does not follow the page's shape: a chat box reads as one. The page's radii still shape
everything else. Enforced by `assistant.test.ts` ("the chat box").
