What an adopter's visitor sees

The same dist/nextgen.js on every page, against the real generated fut-app PARENT registry.

  • Club app — a mock of the adopter's app, in Portuguese, where every route in the registry is a real page. Start here: its Dashboard is the assistant screen. Ask to go somewhere and it takes you there; every other page stays as it was, with the small orb to bring you back.
  • Plain — a host page with no CSS at all.
  • Themed, left nav — declares its own --nextgen-accent; the orb should wait on the right.
  • Themed, right nav — the mirror image; the orb should wait on the left.

Voice. The microphone works on localhost. Speech becomes text only where the browser can do that on the device; elsewhere the orb still reacts to your voice and asks you to type.
